# OLYMPUS

A hard science-fiction soldier's life for the browser, named for Olympus Mons. You are a career Martian marine: barracks, mess hall, drills and the range on Mars under 0.38 g; the shuttle to Phobos, days under thrust to Jupiter, peacekeeping under Europa's ice, ship combat and boarding seen from inside a strapped-in body, and special operations later. High-resolution pixel art, top-down at several scales, realism where it makes the game better. The solar system above you runs as in LONGHAUL (`../longhaul`); the body, the rifle and the dice play as in tradecraft (`../tradecraft`).

## Play

- **Mac:** `./play.sh` serves your play copy (a release, pinned) on http://localhost:8200 and opens it. `tools/promote.sh` moves it to the latest release (`--status` says where it stands; `tools/promote.sh v0.0.1` rolls back).
- **Windows:** `play.cmd` (Git and Python 3 needed). `play.cmd --promote` moves to the latest release, `play.cmd --status` says where you are.
- **The look gallery:** `./play.sh --gallery` (or `play.cmd --gallery`) opens the samples on :8212 to star and score; "Copy my picks" gives the text to paste back.
- Nothing to install beyond a browser and Python 3. The game is plain ES modules with three.js vendored, and runs offline.

## Where it stands

0.0.x, the foundations: the design (`docs/DESIGN.md`, the hub, and `docs/design/*.md`), the engine core, the pixel stage, the figures and the first look samples (0.0.1), the skeleton (0.0.2, built from `docs/build/0.0.2.md`), a walkable barracks on a laid-out base (0.0.3, `docs/build/0.0.3.md`), the body's needs with the first skip (0.0.4, `docs/build/0.0.4.md`), and the rifle at the 25 m line: weapon handling, stop motion and tradecraft's shot (0.0.5, `docs/build/0.0.5.md`), with a patch round on your notes about 0.0.4: the eyes adapting to the dark, the rack's slower pace, the stairs marked and the floor named; then a patch round on your two asks of 2026-11-06 (0.0.6, `docs/build/0.0.6.md`): R saying what it does, single actions and a menu that chains them, and regulation haircuts that grow; then the line at 300 m (0.0.7, `docs/build/0.0.7.md`): the KD range in full, the round's flight in Melas's air, the zero, the wind, the far window, the relay, range safety in full and the wound model; then Pairs (0.0.8, `docs/build/0.0.8.md`), the first piece of close-quarters battle: targets that turn on the 25 m bay, the marine's own take-in before time stops, the double and triple taps as rows priced before you press, steel that rings, and the reloads named by their purpose.

What you can do now:
- **Live a sol in Echo Company's barracks.** New career puts you at the foot of your rack in bay-2-1 on Monday, Sol 590 of MY 211, at 12:39 Melas time, your squad at their racks. Z Q S D walk by physical key (1.14 m/s), Shift runs (2.69) while your body can, Alt strolls (0.75). The game is drawn at 16 px/m with the chibi figures, your pick from the look round. The barracks is built whole on both floors (twelve squad bays in three layouts, heads, offices, the quarterdeck, three stairs, each flight climbed only from its foot through the opening in its rail, the floor changing once you near its top, "UP" painted near a flight's top and "DOWN" at a well's bottom with a walking arrow from where you step on, a night light over each, the watch naming the floor), the same in every career: the world is handcrafted.
- **Have a body.** Hunger, thirst, the bladder and the bowel, grime and stubble, your hands' dirt (the head soils them, a sink or the shower washes them), the hair growing since the barber, sleep and the body clock, stamina and stress run every second, lived or skipped; the needs that have left fine fold into the watch, and C opens PERSONNEL: your figure as you are (wet hair, grime, a tired face), what you feel in words, never a number, and your file.
- **Use what is at hand with R.** A shower stall, a sink, the head, a toilet stall, your rack, your locker, a tap, the hall's drinking fountain, the machines and the barber's pole: R's plate on the instinct line names the one action R does and its time before you press ("R WASH HANDS · 30 S", "R SHAVE · 4 MIN, DONE 19:46"), on every line, the klaxon's included. Every action is single, and R picks the most urgent: at a sink wash your hands if they are dirty, else drink if thirsty, else shave where stubble shows. R held opens a menu in the game's pixels of every action there, each with its time or why it can't be done ("SHAVE (CLEAN-SHAVEN)"), a drop-down where it has a choice (how long to sleep, which outfit, which cut): check several (digits and Space, or the mouse) and Enter runs them in order behind one splash, the total and the end shown first. A door opens and closes, in 0.4 s (a weather door in 0.6); away from anything, R twice stands by for 15 minutes.
- **Skip time behind the splash.** Anything over 10 s goes behind it (a hand wash or a drink in under a second); from 3 minutes it zooms in on you, closes an iris over what you are doing (the stall's steam, the sink's foam, the sleeper's breathing), runs the watch fast and shows what changed before zooming back out: ten minutes of shower in 4.3 s. Space goes to the end, Backspace stops where you are ("finish your shower · 8 min", in what you wore into it), or in a chain after the running step.
- **Sleep, and get up for the klaxon.** Taps at 22:00, reveille at 05:30 (07:00 at the weekend) with the bugle; one night a week, at a minute drawn from the world's seed between 00:30 and 04:30, the alert drill: up, utilities (dressed at the alarm's tempo, 2.6 minutes), the foot of your rack before the count five minutes on, and stand fast there until the stand-down. Your squad sits up, climbs down, dresses, undresses and lies down again in front of you, never popping, rising while you do. Getting in and out of the rack takes its time (4 s from a lower berth at the klaxon, more when nothing hurries), and R at your rack lies you down in sight before the night, a move key taking it back. You sleep in what you wear: R held at your rack chains dress (PT kit) and sleep until reveille.
- **Let your eyes adapt.** Just in from a lit room the night bay is dark and you see only round you; five minutes in it is as 0.0.4 showed it, and after half an hour clearly lighter, the fog of awareness following the same number. The lights coming on dazzle you for a few seconds, and the fire watch's red lamp spares your night vision.
- **Hear the drill instructor in your head.** One line at a time, its tone as its border; T does what it says where you stand (get up, dress, drink, shower, wash your hands after the head, shave), T held the next thing it would say.
- **Keep your hair in regulation.** Your cut grows 0.4 mm a day, lived or skipped, drawn on your figure and the doll at four steps: fresh, growing out (3 sols), due (7: the DI says "Your cut is due Wednesday.") and out of regulation (10). The barber's pole at the PX, 10:00 to 20:00, gives a haircut in 12 minutes; a shaved head is buzzed at a sink in 10. C's Haircut row says where you stand, never a number.
- **Walk out onto Base Melas.** Through the front door to the company street, and across the base (37 buildings, the parade deck, the roads and paths, the regolith between) to its fence; the four gates stay shut this release.
- **Watch the sol turn.** The lights follow the hour and the sun clears the canyon's wall at 05:44 at the start's sol, its disc coming up over a minute and a half; the wall clocks agree with your watch.
- **Meet your squad, generated.** Everyone in Echo Company, you included, is drawn from the world's seed: names, looks (the Corps' regulation cuts, a thinning man's head shaved), ranks, ages, homes, traits. Out of sight they are drawn as you last saw them, and change only where you can't see them, but for the moves they make in front of you.
- **See what you are aware of.** The fog of awareness is perception's own sight function, cell for cell: a cone ahead, a ring round you, reach by the light as your eyes take it; woken in your rack you see past its foot, and your own berth is always in sight.
- **Hear the barracks.** Footsteps by surface, doors, the shower's water, the tap, the flush, the bugle and the klaxon through the walls, all made by code, quieter in Melas's thin air; the volume and mute in the Esc menu.
- **Shoot the 25 m line in stop motion.** Tests on the title, "Show clear" (`?play=test-handling`): the KD range's 25 m bay outside the East gate, the same in every career, alone on your lane with an R-7 slung and three magazines of five; a generated coach at your shoulder and a generated range officer on the tower, giving the Corps' commands as the line is ready (prone, LOAD, MAKE READY, three strings of five prone, kneeling and standing, CEASE FIRE, RELOAD, UNLOAD, SHOW CLEAR). G brings the rifle up and time stands still while you choose; each step plays for its time and rolls Weapon handling (routine for the trained, marked so), the panel offering only what you believe the rifle allows, the weapon card drawing it as you believe it. Each shot is tradecraft's: a d20 against the 25 m card's DC 19 with every modifier (prone +4, the held aim to +4, the optic +1), the same list on the plaque over the card, in the log and in the hole on the paper. A stoppage the coach set (a dummy round, a light load or a double feed, drawn from the world's seed) clicks or jams in your second or third magazine: look, then clear it by its steps. Range safety is the Corps' (the muzzle, the box, the cold line, the finger, the tower first): the coach warns, then stops the line. The course never dead-ends: a shooter with no rounds left sits the string out (the coach checks and says so; the string goes down as not fired), a command not answered in a minute of lived time is passed over with a fault, and at SHOW CLEAR the coach clears a rifle never shown himself, so it always reaches the end card. One magazine a string: a change before the tower's RELOAD is marked on its row and counted a fault.
- **The weapon keys.** G rifle up (with no rifle, v0.0.4's G as before), X in or out of stop motion, the digits the panel's steps (tapped at the normal tempo, with Shift rushed, held careful; Fire held holds the aim, then fires), A and W the stance up and down, V the carry, Enter aims at the bracketed card, Q and D move the brackets, the mouse aims and fires; I shows what you carry, believed counts only; J folds each roll to one line that opens to its sum.
- **Reach for a magazine and see what it holds.** The panel's "Reach: chest pouch 1 (5 ball · front of 2)", "Reach: the dump pouch", "Reach: at your feet" bring one to your support hand (0.69 s at Weapon handling 10, a pickup off the ground 1.0 s), its weight felt at once (none, a few, about half, nearly full, full); "Look at it" reads its witness holes and its top round ("5 rounds, tracer on top: a red tip"); then Magazine in (0.92 s: the reach and the seat are v0.0.5's magazine in, 1.4 s of base, split in two, so LOAD and the RELOADs take what they took), Put back, Dump it or Drop it. The drills reach by rule, the course's kind first, the fullest believed, never an empty. Rounds come in kinds (ball, tracer); the weapon card draws the magazine in your hand as you believe it, I lists each pouch by name with its magazines' counts and kinds, and `?play=test-handling&setup=mixed` adds a fourth magazine, of tracer, its kind not told.
- **Shoot the line at 300 m.** Tests on the title, "The line at 300 m" (`?play=test-range`; `&setup=confirm`, `s200` or `far` to start part-way): the KD range in full beside the 25 m bay, five of your squad firing their own strings on the lanes beside yours, a generated coach, range officer, corpsman and pits detail. The tower's command you have still to answer stands in the radio box, marked NOW, until you have done it, and names the point you stand on ("Point eleven, LOAD."), your numbered stake marked with a brass frame, here and on the 25 m bay; its 60 s wait counts only the time it waits on you alone, never the relay's. Zero at 25 m in three-round groups the coach reads and calls ("6 down, 14 right"), dialled on the turrets (Z S ↑ ↓ a click of elevation, Shift with Q D of windage), the plate and the weapon card saying "ZEROING ▲4 ◀2" (the clicks dialled in this zero) until the coach accepts it; behind the walk's splash, the 300 m line prone at 4×, the dope for the line, the flags read (a row of four stands 18 m downrange of each line, one always in clear view, five red and white bands filled one per 1.5 m/s with a shadow on the ground; the far window keeps off the one you read) or the coach's call, and the hold for them, five rounds slow fire, each marked by the pits in the far window before the next ("Wait for the mark"; a round at a face down in the pits goes into the berm): the face down, the spotter in the hole, the face up with its score disk, the coach's word on it. The plaque gives the chance of a five or a V. The pits behind their splash; ten rounds rapid sitting at 200 m in 60 s with the magazine change inside the string and the coach's dummy among them, the far window held on your face while the pits mark the string and the coach reads it; police your brass, the rounds back to the coach, "No brass, no ammo", the card, with the relay's scores ranked beside yours. About 28 game minutes, 5 played.
- **See it land in the far window.** Aimed at a far target, bracketing it, or with E held on a KD line, a round window opens at the frame's edge toward it, labelled "TARGET 8 · 300 M", the SO-4's reticle at 4× with your hold, and at 1× the naked eye's view: a quarter of the scale, four times the ground, no reticle, the lever easing between them over 0.5 s; the plaque gives the chance of a scoring hit on the strike you believe, its terms as glyphs (range, the wind as read, the hold, the turrets off your zero) and the rings' odds; the log says after the shot what moved it.
- **Keep the range safe.** The tower's commands, the cold line, the muzzle, forward of the line, CEASE FIRE; a negligent discharge lands somewhere real (the berm, the ground, a foot, a neighbour), and one that hits a person is the corpsman's and the aid station's, the range closed and the card saying what became of them. A wound bleeds, hurts, knocks you down and costs you the rolls, said in words on C and by the DI.
- **Hear the range.** The report through electronic ear defenders, the berm's slap-back 0.17 s after, the action's clicks at their moments, each case's tick where it lands, the tower's chime before its lines; nothing while time stands still. At distance, the crack of your round leaving and its strike's thump 1.35 s later at 300 m, the relay's reports a sound's way late, the carriers in the pits.
- **Take in a target that turns, at your marine's speed.** Tests on the title, "Pairs" (`?play=test-pairs&tier=recruit|private|sgt|raider|tierone`): the 25 m bay with a turning target in front of each frame and lines painted at 7 and 10 m, the R-7 with magazines of 10, 10 and 2, and a generated coach running five strings on his shot timer. Pick a sheet first (boot recruit, private, sergeant, Raider, tier one) and wait out of stop motion at low ready: when a target turns, the world runs on at 1:1 while amber brackets close round it over your marine's own take-in (0.65 s for the recruit in his sector, 0.55 for the private, 0.05 for the tier one; twice that or more when it turns outside where the muzzle points, a little more for two or three at once), every key but Space and Esc waiting (and for 0.35 s after the pause, so a key pressed by reflex at the turn never takes it away); every paper target is the same khaki board until he has taken it in, what it holds painted only once he has seen it; then time stops with "Contact · 0.55 s.", the stamp over the target and what he saw ("a rifle", "a phone in his hand", "can't see his hands"). The shot rows are priced and rolled before you press: Pair, Hammer, Triple, Failure drill and To the head, each with its time and every round's odds, struck where your Marksmanship or your rung does not reach; Challenge and Hold him lead on a no-shoot. Below rung 2 the panel at the pause is step by step: Off safe, Aim, then the shot. One round in a paper man is not "still up" (it takes two); the armoured silhouette rings on its plate, rocking, until the head plate falls to the ground ("He's still up. Head."); the two-round magazine runs dry and RELOAD leads the panel; after the last string the coach hands you a fresh ten for the lull's Top up. After each string the coach's paper reads "TURN TO PAUSE 0.55 · PAUSE TO FIRST 1.50 · SPLITS 0.40 · 2 A" (the steel's "1 RING · 1 HEAD DOWN"), and the end card offers "Again as …" each other sheet on the same seed (the same targets at the same gaps), listing this session's runs on the seed one under another.
- **Play the moments to score.** Tests on the title: "Ten minutes under the shower", "The klaxon at 02:30", "Show clear" (and its mixed magazines), "The line at 300 m" and "Pairs", each with a score card and Copy my scores to paste back.
- **Measure it.** `?bench=squadbay-night` plays the bay at 23:00 for 20 s, `?bench=splash-sleep` the night's splash, `?bench=range-line` fifteen shots on the 25 m line, `?bench=range-far` the 300 m line with the far window open, and each gives the numbers to copy back (60 fps on the Mac).

Under it: one sim put together in `src/sim/game.js` (nine domains, the body and the weapons among them; activities lived, fast-forwarded or skipped to their outcome on one minute grid, so lived and skipped give the same body and land on the same second); saves per world (store v2), and a save made anywhere, mid-splash and in stop motion included, loads and carries on byte for byte, in node and in the page; a v0.0.3 save loads with its body filled in, a v0.0.4 save as it stands, a v0.0.5 save with its cuts mapped to the regulation ones; a game with no rifle in it is made as v0.0.4's but for the hair and the hands (0.0.6), and the rifle changes nothing in it as it plays; a chain of actions is one skip whose steps change at the same millisecond lived, fast-forwarded or skipped; the gun is a truth and its holder's belief, the HUD reading only the belief; the eyes' adaptation one number for the picture's exposure and the sight's reach, lived and skipped alike; A* on 0.25 m cells for three body widths; the stage paints areas and figures in a worker and holds uploads to 1 MB a frame.

The roadmap to 1.0 is `docs/design/ROADMAP.md`; what each release changed is `CHANGELOG.md`.
